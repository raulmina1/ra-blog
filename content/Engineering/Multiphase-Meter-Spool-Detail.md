# The Spool That Measures Everything

> A short, flanged section of pipe that gives a multiphase meter a stable place to read gas, water, and crude — all in one pass.

## What a Spool Is

A **spool** is a short, pre-fabricated section of pipe with **flanges on both ends**. It's built and pressure-tested in the workshop, then inserted into a line in the field like a plug-in module. Instead of cutting and welding pipe on site, you bolt the spool in place — faster, cleaner, and easier to maintain.

This spool was designed as the **host for a multiphase meter** — a compact run that locates the metering point at the exact place it needs to sit.

## Why a Multiphase Meter Needs Its Own Section

A **multiphase meter** measures oil, water, and gas at the same time, without separating them first — a big advantage over traditional three-phase separators. But it only reads accurately if the flow reaching it is stable and well developed. That means:

- a **straight section** of pipe upstream so the flow profile evens out,
- a **rigid, predictable run** so the meter sees consistent conditions,
- exact **connection locations** so the instrument ties in where the drawing says.

That is precisely what this spool provides — a controlled, meter-ready pocket in the line.

## The Weldolet Detail

The "WELDOLET DETALLE A" view is the **branch connection** on the spool — where the meter's line ties into the main pipe.

- It's a **welded branch fitting** (a weldolet) instead of a plain cut-and-weld tee, because a weldolet distributes stress better under internal pressure.
- The detail sheet defines the **weld geometry and reinforcement** — a branch point is one of the highest-stress spots on any pressurized line, so this is where a drawing earns its keep.
- Get this wrong and the branch becomes a hidden failure point; get it right and the connection is as strong as the pipe itself.

## Reading the Drawing

The plan has everything a fabricator needs:

- **VISTA FRONTAL — UBICACIÓN DE CONEXIONES** ("front view — connection locations"): where each instrument connection sits on the spool.
- **WELDOLET DETALLE A**: the branch detail, with the weld and reinforcement called out.
- **Dual-unit dimensions** in both **inches and millimetres** (for example a total height of 7.359 in / 186.944 mm, and a 22.25 in / 565.15 mm width) — the standard practice for international fabrication and QA.

## Where This Lives in the Field

Spools like this are everyday hardware in **well testing and production metering** — the measurement layer that tells an operator how much oil, water, and gas a well is really producing.

> [!note] RA
> A spool is a small piece of pipe, but it carries the measurement that decides whether a well makes money. Good design is obvious; great design is transparent — every detail holds the pressure so the data holds the truth.
