## 🤖 Anthropic ships office software during its IPO roadshow, and Microsoft feels it

During the roadshow Anthropic released Claude Whiteboards and Claude Sheets in beta, moved Claude Dashboards to GA as Claude Data on all paid plans, launched Claude for Manufacturers (after healthcare, financial services, legal and government), and put Claude Meetings into beta on Team and Enterprise — Claude joins a Google Meet call as a visible participant, takes notes and builds shared artefacts.

**Why it matters.** The competitive line has moved from model quality to the seat: agents entering meetings and spreadsheets attack the per-user licence, which is where Microsoft's margin actually sits. Two data points to keep: Anthropic is shipping vertical products faster than it is shipping benchmarks, and the market now prices that as a Microsoft risk.

## In brief

### 🤖 AI & Models

**A thousand agents, Git and one Postgres: Anthropic formalises a proof in 28.7 hours**

An early build of Fable 5.2 ran 1 lead agent and 999 helpers to formalise Fermat's Last Theorem in Lean: 28.7 hours, one-third the output tokens and a proof one-seventh the length of the September run, which took 11 days with researchers directing dozens of agents.

*Why it matters:* This is the opposite design to the "every user gets their own agent" pattern: shared state plus Git as the coordination protocol beats message-passing.

<sub>Anthropic developer blog (anthropic.com/engineering) · 09.11.2026 — [read the source](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering)</sub>

**Distribution is the new leaderboard: Hydrogen becomes the default, Copilot retires two models**

Gemini 4 Hydrogen (\$1.50/\$7.50 per million tokens, 1M context) is now the default model in Search's AI Mode worldwide and free in the Gemini app, replacing Gemini 3.8 Flash. GitHub Copilot will retire Gemini 3.8 Flash and Gemini 4 Argon on 14.12., replacing both with Hydrogen and Carbon.

*Why it matters:* A model's position now depends as much on being the default as on its index score, and any workflow that pins a model ID breaks on 14.12.

### ⚡ Energy & Markets

**Germany's 2027 budget: €560.9bn of spending, ~€210bn of new debt**

The Bundestag Budget Committee finished at 5:55 on Friday after nearly 16 hours: spending of €560.9bn with net new borrowing of almost €123bn in the core budget alone.

*Why it matters:* The electricity tax is the one line here that hits Raúl's own budget, and it is now a Tuesday decision.

<sub>DW / FAZ / Deutschlandfunk · 14.11.2026 — [read the source](https://www.dw.com/en/germanys-2027-budget-a-record-561-billion-and-a-mountain-of-debt/a-79943216)</sub>

**Two years of the core network: 537 km of 9,040, and the state holds the risk**

Two years after approval, 537 km of the 9,040 km core network are in operation, with 900 km more planned by end-2027 (507 km were retrofitted gas lines; the Lingen–Marl chain runs ~120 km and delivers real hydrogen).

*Why it matters:* The CCfD round is the only mechanism that currently converts political ambition into bankable offtake for industrial H2, so its timing matters more than another kilometre of pipe.

### 📊 Money & Policy

**Ecopetrol's refining turnaround: from a COP 0.97trn loss to COP 4.23trn profit in nine months**

January–September: net profit COP 13.7trn (+82.7 % y/y), revenue COP 103.9trn (+14.3 %), EBITDA COP 47trn.

*Why it matters:* The margin, not the volume, is the whole story: Brent averaging \$88.9 vs \$69.9 a year earlier did the work, which means the same refining result reverses if crude falls back below \$80.

### 🌍 Geopolitics & Conflict

**Iran says three tankers hit mines on Saturday; the US says the lanes are clear**

Iran's navy and the Revolutionary Guard said three oil tankers caught fire after hitting naval mines in the Strait of Hormuz on Saturday, one in the eastern strait in the morning and two more in the evening that were "attempting to slip through a prohibited route".

*Why it matters:* The claims now describe a policy of passage (violators burn) rather than single incidents, which is what insurers price.
