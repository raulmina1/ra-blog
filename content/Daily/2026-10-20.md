## ⚡ Brent settles \$109.55 after swinging \$3 lower and back; WTI jumps 2.2 % to \$103.66 into expiry and the Brent-WTI spread halves

Brent for December settled 31 cents lower at \$109.55 — it fell more than \$3 intraday on profit-taking after Friday's five-month high of \$109.86, then recovered — while WTI for November, expiring today, jumped \$2.25 or 2.2 % to \$103.66, its highest settlement since 15 September; the more liquid WTI December contract settled at \$98.54, down 0.5 %.

**Why it matters.** The two benchmarks are telling different stories and both are true: waterborne crude is relieved by the Russian licence, domestic US crude is squeezed by the Hormuz disruption plus Hurricane Isaias shut-ins and inventories ~3 % below the five-year average. A \$5.89 spread is the price of that split.

### 🤖 AI & Models

**Microsoft walks away from Britain's largest AI data centre because the grid connection slid to the 2030s**

Microsoft terminated its involvement in Nscale's £2bn Loughton, Essex project — planned as "the UK's largest supercomputer" and the anchor of Microsoft's \$15bn UK AI infrastructure pledge — after the site's national-grid connection was pushed from end-2026 to the 2030s.

*Why it matters:* This is the clearest evidence yet that the 2027 build-out is constrained by power and grid interconnection, not by chips or capital.

### ⚡ Energy & Markets

**Germany replaces its storage mandates with a 24 TWh strategic gas reserve — financed by a new levy from 1 October 2027**

The economics ministry's draft law for a strategic gas reserve, sent to associations on 13 October with comments due 19 October at noon, lets the existing national storage-filling mandates expire on 31 March 2027 and replaces them with a state crisis reserve.

*Why it matters:* The cost is small and the structural change is not: Germany is admitting that filling mandates do not survive a gas market this tight and is buying insurance instead.

**The 2027 budget stalls in the Chamber as the Liberal Party breaks quorum — with the legal deadline at midnight tonight**

The Senate plenary advanced through roughly 90 articles of the COP 634.9 trillion 2027 budget on Monday — including a block of 65 approved 55–17 and a second block of 11 approved 56–16 — while the Chamber of Representatives lost quorum in the afternoon and recessed until 8 p.m.

*Why it matters:* This is the scenario the market has been pricing since the IMF's "not sustainable" verdict: a budget that cannot pass becomes a decree.

### 🌍 Geopolitics & Conflict

**Washington Post: Tehran is deliberately escalating before 3 November to inflict maximum oil-price pain on Trump**

Iranian officials and regional diplomats told the Washington Post that Tehran sees the weeks before the 3 November US midterms as its critical leverage window, on the calculation that Trump will be less sensitive to high energy prices afterwards.

*Why it matters:* ⏱️ If the escalation is calendar-driven, the oil risk premium has a defined expiry and a defined peak, which is tradeable information rather than background noise.

**Lavrov flies to Tehran for the first time since February 2025 and pitches a Russian-Chinese "collective security" architecture for the Gulf**

Sergey Lavrov met Abbas Araghchi in Tehran on Monday — his first visit to Iran since February 2025 — and said Russia is committed to the 20-year strategic partnership that entered into force on 2 October, with Russian specialists gradually returning to the Bushehr nuclear plant.

*Why it matters:* Moscow is positioning itself as the broker of the waterway, and the GCC is letting it try — which is a shift from the earlier US-only channel.
