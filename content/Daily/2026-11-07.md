## 🤖 Anthropic's IPO is now priced by the market, not by the company: \$1.0–1.25 trillion is the new talk

Stakeholders told CNBC that \$1.5 trillion is now a "stretch", with one investor calling \$1.0–1.25 trillion the realistic target — down from the ~\$2 trillion discussed when Anthropic filed confidentially. The WSJ reports three funds pushing for a lower range; Polymarket moved the odds of a November listing sharply toward December.

**Why it matters.** This is a financing story with a feedback loop: OpenAI reportedly closed only \$11 bn of a targeted \$30 bn at a \$1.25 trillion valuation, Microsoft tightened oversight of Nebius, IREN and Nscale (Bloomberg), and CoreWeave/SpaceX default swaps hit records while the neoclouds lost half their value in a month.

### ⚡ Energy & Markets

**Brent ends the week at \$98.32 after a 13 % weekly collapse — the steepest since the start of the pandemic**

Brent's January contract settled \$98.32 (+\$0.03, +0.03 %) and WTI December \$90.83 (+0.31, +0.34 %), both essentially flat on the day after a brutal week: Brent −13.3 % (\$113.43 → \$98.32) and WTI −12.8 % (\$104.12 → \$90.83).

*Why it matters:* The week repriced the *war premium*, not the barrel: Middle Eastern exports are recovering, demand signals are weak, and the midterms raised the odds of a settlement.

<sub>Reuters / CNBC / LSEG · 06.11.2026 — [read the source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/06/oil-price-today-brent-wti.html)</sub>

**German industry grew again in September — and the eurozone consumer did not**

German industrial production rose +0.7 % m/m in September, driven by automobiles +17.1 % and pharma +8.0 %, while machinery fell 6.5 % and construction 2.6 %; over Q3 production was still −0.4 % below Q2.

*Why it matters:* The two prints point in opposite directions and both are weak: the industrial gain is a rebound from shutdowns and model changeovers (autos +17.1 % off a depressed base), while the consumer is shrinking.
