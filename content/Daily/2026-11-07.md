## ⚡ Brent ends the week at \$98.32 after a 13 % weekly collapse — the steepest since the start of the pandemic

Brent's January contract settled \$98.32 (+\$0.03, +0.03 %) and WTI December \$90.83 (+0.31, +0.34 %), both essentially flat on the day after a brutal week: Brent −13.3 % (\$113.43 → \$98.32) and WTI −12.8 % (\$104.12 → \$90.83) — per LSEG data back to 1988 the biggest absolute weekly fall on record and the steepest percentage drop since the start of COVID. The Brent-WTI spread sits at \$7.49; Brent is ~22 % below its 52-week high of \$126.41 but still +61.85 % year to date.

**Why it matters.** The week repriced the *war premium*, not the barrel: Middle Eastern exports are recovering, demand signals are weak, and the midterms raised the odds of a settlement.

### 🤖 AI & Models

**Anthropic's IPO is now priced by the market, not by the company: \$1.0–1.25 trillion is the new talk**

Stakeholders told CNBC that \$1.5 trillion is now a "stretch", with one investor calling \$1.0–1.25 trillion the realistic target — down from the ~\$2 trillion discussed when Anthropic filed confidentially.

*Why it matters:* This is a financing story with a feedback loop: OpenAI reportedly closed only \$11 bn of a targeted \$30 bn at a \$1.25 trillion valuation, Microsoft tightened oversight of Nebius, IREN and Nscale (Bloomberg).

### 📊 Money & Policy

**The Rescue bill is being costed at more than 4 % of GDP of adjustment by 2030**

Banco de Bogotá's analysis puts the fiscal adjustment contained in the *Ley de Rescate Económico* at more than 4.0 % of GDP by 2030, split between spending cuts and higher taxes, and calls it the largest adjustment in the country's history.

*Why it matters:* This is the number that decides whether the risk premium falls: 4 % of GDP is roughly the size of the credibility gap the IMF flagged, but it depends on a Congress that has already stalled the 2027 budget.

<sub>Banco de Bogotá (research note) · 06.11.2026 — [read the source](https://pbit.bancodebogota.com/Informes/InformesEconomicos.aspx/AnalisisPDF.aspx?PDF=4057)</sub>

**The peso posts its biggest weekly advance since late September — and it happened on one day**

USD/COP closed Friday at 3,239.50 (+9.50, +0.29 %), with the TRM at 3,228.26 (+7.07, +0.22 %); intraday range 3,223.81–3,253.95.

*Why it matters:* The peso's 15.9 % appreciation over twelve months is decelerating precisely because the BanRep decision removed the "hike next" assumption.

<sub>Portafolio / El Espectador / Global66 · 06.11.2026 — [read the source](https://www.portafolio.co/economia/finanzas/dolar-en-colombia-paso-de-3-166-a-3-237-y-registro-su-mayor-avance-semanal-desde-finales-de-septiembre-505895)</sub>

**October payrolls beat by 100,000 — and the December hike is back to ~76 %**

The US added 165,000 jobs in October against ~65,000 expected; unemployment fell to 4.1 % (from 4.2 %) and wages rose 3.1 % y/y versus 2.9 % forecast. Revisions went the same direction: September 29,000 → 60,000 and August to 186,000.

*Why it matters:* This is the print that killed the "labour market is cracking" argument that had supported the dovish side: payrolls, revisions, wages and the unemployment rate all moved together.

<sub>BLS / Axios / Reuters · 06.11.2026 — [read the source](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/06/october-2026-jobs-report-unemployment-165000)</sub>

### 🌍 Geopolitics & Conflict

**The Gulf war has its first confirmed seafarer death — and Iran claims two more tankers on mines**

The Marshall Islands-flagged product tanker MT Velos Sapphire (74,998 dwt, Greek-managed, unladen for Bahrain) was hit by two projectiles ~50 nm north of Qatar's Madinat ash Shamal at 23:20 UTC on 04.11.

*Why it matters:* The pattern changed: Iran is moving from crude tankers to product tankers, tugs, bulkers and dhows (7 vessels since 01.11, only two crude), which spreads risk across the whole cargo mix instead of one segment.

<sub>Reuters / UKMTO / Seatrade Maritime · 06.11.2026 — [read the source](https://www.reuters.com/5f374b32b07b/world/one-seafarer-killed-missile-attack-tanker-gulf-2026-11-06/)</sub>

**Iran has reportedly sent a nuclear proposal to Washington through Qatar — Trump says he will decide soon**

A senior Iranian official said Tehran has sent a nuclear proposal to Washington via Qatar. Trump said he would soon decide whether to pursue a peace agreement or "destroy what remains of Iran".

*Why it matters:* The diplomatic channel exists but has no date attached, and the same week produced both the lowest oil price in a month and a confirmed fatality at sea.
