### ⚡ Energy & Markets

**TTF breaks €100/MWh for the first time since December 2022**

€100.425 at 15:03 GMT, up about 4 % — for the first time since 20 December 2022. The German front-year baseload contract rose 1.7 % to €159.30/MWh, its highest since June 2023, and the French equivalent hit a near two-year high of €122.50.

*Why it matters:* how fast 74.9 % becomes 20 %, not whether prices spike.
