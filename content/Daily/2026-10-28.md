## ⚡ Brent settles at \$113.88 as the war premium leaks — but diesel and freight do not

still near six-month highs after Brent touched \$117 on Monday. Hormuz flows averaged 9.34m bpd over seven days, 54 % of the 17.15m bpd pre-war baseline, while non-Iranian Middle East crude exports held at 16.7m bpd in October versus 16.3m before the war; the East-West pipeline carried 5.6m bpd in September.

**Why it matters.** refining and freight costs, plus diesel-export demand, are the binding constraints now. Global visible inventories are at multi-year lows after roughly 500m barrels drawn since February, and government stock releases are largely finished.
