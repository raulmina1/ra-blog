## 🤖 Claude can now read your bank accounts — read-only, and that is the whole point

Claude Personal Finance launched in beta for US Pro, Max and Ultra subscribers: up to 16 US banks connect through Plaid, Claude reads up to two years of transactions, and it cannot move, manage or invest any money. Hub chats are excluded from training, and Anthropic states there are no ads or sponsored recommendations.

**Why it matters.** This is the settlement of the fight from 22.10, when Morgan Stanley blocked third-party agents and JPM/BofA/Citi/WFC restricted them: the agent gets aggregation, not execution. For Europe there is no launch and no Plaid equivalent at this depth, so the product is a US-only permission model for now.

### ⚡ Energy & Markets

**Week ahead: a \$20 peace dividend already paid, and an asymmetric calendar**

The base case (70 % probability) is Brent consolidating at \$98–106 while the market waits for concrete US–Iran signals; a ceasefire framework or sanctions relief would take it to \$93–95, while failed talks or new tanker attacks push it to \$105–110.

*Why it matters:* The market has already paid a ~\$20 "peace dividend" on a negotiation that has no date; the forecast's own asymmetry (upside on headline risk, downside only on a signed framework) is the thing to hold.

### 🌍 Geopolitics & Conflict

**Herzog hands Eisenkot the mandate — Netanyahu's first real exit since 2021**

President Isaac Herzog tasked Gadi Eisenkot (Yashar) with forming the next government on Saturday evening, with the formal ceremony on Sunday; in the consultations 67 MKs recommended Eisenkot against 46 for Netanyahu.

*Why it matters:* This is the closest Netanyahu has been to losing office since 2021, and the arithmetic forces Eisenkot to break a campaign promise on either the ultra-Orthodox or the Arab parties.
