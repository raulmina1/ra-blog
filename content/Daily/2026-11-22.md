### ⚡ Energy & Markets

**EIA: chokepoint shipments +19% in Q3, October output at 23.1 mb/d — and \$86 Brent for 2027**

the highest since the conflict began — with flows through Hormuz and Suez rising and Bab el-Mandeb falling; Vortexa data shows October near Q3 levels. The agency expects Brent to average \$86/bbl in 2027 as inventories rebuild and demand stays flat.

*Why it matters:* 2027 baselines derived from an external capacity audit can move quotas in ways quotas have not moved since the war began.

<sub>Reuters / EIA · 19–20.11.2026 — [read the source](https://finance.yahoo.com/energy/articles/oil-prices-retreat-traders-assess-123700195.html)</sub>

### 📊 Money & Policy

**Ecopetrol negotiates 10-year gas contracts with PDVSA and waits on an OFAC licence**

> What? Ecopetrol is pursuing 10-year supply contracts with PDVSA and an OFAC licence to import Venezuelan gas through the 224 km Antonio Ricaurte pipeline (88 km Colombian, 137 km Venezuelan).

*Why it matters:* licence, rehabilitation, Venezuelan production, payment guarantees — is unresolved, so this is a 2027 option at best, not a winter fix.

<sub>NoticiasBQ / El Espectador (Bloomberg) · 20.11.2026 — [read the source](https://noticiasbq.com/gas-de-venezuela-podria-ser-75-mas-barato-ecopetrol-busca-importar-desde-pdvsa/)</sub>

**The OECD hands Colombia the number the tax reform has to close: 7.2% deficit**

roughly double the central bank's 3% target — and a central-government deficit of 7.2% of GDP in 2026 with public debt still rising.

*Why it matters:* private capital, higher and predictable carbon prices and risk mitigation are required, which is the same message the gas and oil chapters are getting.

<sub>OECD Economic Survey of Colombia · 20.11.2026 — [read the source](https://www.oecd.org/es/about/news/press-releases/2026/11/stronger-public-finances-and-higher-productivity-are-key-to-sustained-growth-in-colombia.html)</sub>
