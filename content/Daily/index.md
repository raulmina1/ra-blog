# 🗞️ Daily Brief

> The information I want to consume every day — finance, energy, geopolitics and the model layer — one issue, published by itself every morning.

A feed forgets. This does not: every issue is filed into **threads** (one per topic), and each thread accumulates the story of that topic week after week. The garden's graph grows by one node and a handful of edges every day.

Longer investigations built from the same sources live under **Research** — one question, one dataset, official figures only.
