## 🤖 Google ships a "Decisions API": 1,000 graded questions in one pass, \$0.10 per million input tokens

> What? Google introduced the Gemini Decisions API with a purpose-built Gemini Decision 1 model that scores candidate answers instead of generating text: yes/no probabilities, multiple choice over 2–255 options, or rubric scoring, one shared multimodal context of up to 256K tokens, up to 1,000 questions per request, 150–250 ms end-to-end, billed at \$0.10/M input with no output tokens.

**The core grid is being built faster than the market that will use it**

FNB Gas' first stocktake: more than 500 km of the hydrogen core network are operational, of which 507 km are converted gas pipelines; the largest section is the 400 km Lubmin–Bobbau line (Gascade, filled December 2025).
