## 🤖 OpenAI puts verified business profiles and agents inside ChatGPT

OpenAI launched Businesses in ChatGPT in beta: companies claim a blue-check profile and deploy Business Agents that answer product questions, initially limited to eligible US e-commerce and online-service firms. Why it matters — Same day as Anthropic's workspace launch, same logic in reverse: instead of moving work into Claude, OpenAI moves commerce into the chat window.

### ⚡ Energy & Markets

**Brent breaks \$90 intraday, settles at \$91.49 — then bounces on tanker attacks**

Brent for January settled \$91.49 on Tuesday (−1.17 %), after touching \$89.16 — the first sub-\$90 print since 31 August; WTI December \$86.04 (−0.93 %). By 01:19 GMT on Wednesday Brent had recovered to \$92.20 (+0.78 %) and WTI to \$86.53, on renewed attacks on shipping.

### 📊 Money & Policy

**October inflation 6.30 % — and the peso is back at 3,270**

DANE reported October CPI at 6.30 % annual (September 6.29 %), below the 6.38 % Citi consensus; +0.20 % m/m and 5.94 % year-to-date. Core ex-food fell to 6.07 % (second consecutive decline), services to 7.66 %; restaurants and hotels lead at 9.39 % annual, education 7.44 %, health 7.37 %.
