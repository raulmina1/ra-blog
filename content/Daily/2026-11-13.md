## 🤖 Gemini 4 Hydrogen becomes Google's volume default at \$1.50/\$7.50

Google launched Gemini 4 Hydrogen (gemini-4-hydrogen), replacing Gemini 3.8 Flash as the high-volume default. Price: \$1.50 / \$7.50 per million tokens, flat across the full 1M-token context; a new "xhigh" thinking tier sits above the old reasoning levels.

**Why it matters.** For agent loops that fire hundreds of small requests per task, flat pricing over 1M tokens is the difference between estimate and gamble. Caveat: the benchmark numbers come from press/AI briefings, not from a published AA page — treat 51 as approximate until AA lists the configuration.

### ⚡ Energy & Markets

**Berlin opens another capacity auction — skewed to hydrogen-ready gas plants**

Germany launched a new capacity-market auction whose design is likely to favour H₂-ready gas-fired power plants; the previous round awarded €12bn in contracts to 11 H₂-ready gas plants earlier this month.

*Why it matters:* This is how Berlin is buying dispatchable capacity now: gas first, H₂ conversion later.

### 📊 Money & Policy

**Colombia's Q3 GDP lands Tuesday — consensus is "below 2 %", the weakest of 2026**

DANE publishes Q3 GDP on Tuesday 17.11. at 10:00: Banco de Bogotá sees below 2 %, Grupo Cibest's NowCast 2.1 %, BanRep's July forecast 2.2 % — against 3.5 % in Q2.
