## 🤖 Anthropic rewrote its Messages API in Rust and used the old service as the verifier

Anthropic published on 25 November how it moved the Messages API — the entry point for customers as well as its own Claude Code and Cowork apps — from Python to Rust. Traffic on that path grew roughly 1,800× between early 2024 and autumn 2026; the company reports about 70 % less CPU and more than 80 % less memory per request, with a roughly 20 % faster median time to first token and no downtime.

**Why it matters.** Verification, not generation, is now the binding constraint on engineering ambition. The transferable pattern is treating the running legacy system as an oracle and turning "where it differs" into a signal an agent can act on — which is exactly the infrastructure layer that decides how far agent-built software can go inside companies.

### 🤖 AI & Models

**Google makes its MCP servers the only door into Workspace for agents**

Google made its official Model Context Protocol servers and a Workspace CLI generally available for Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Chat and Tasks.

*Why it matters:* Platform access policy, not model quality, is becoming the cost driver for agent vendors: retention of embeddings is capped, quotas are per product, and admins get DLP and audit logs over agent actions.

**First firm-level evidence: AI mostly adds output, rarely removes a job**

Anthropic's economics team interviewed 66 people at 58 US companies using its products and published the results on 23 November.

*Why it matters:* This is the first qualitative dataset behind the macro argument about AI and employment, and it lands on the "output, not headcount" side.

### ⚡ Energy & Markets

**Brent settles at \$96.57, its highest since 6 November, as OPEC+ looks set to roll over**

Brent settled up \$1.96, or 2.07 %, at \$96.57 a barrel on Thursday, the highest close since 6 November, after Iran's Revolutionary Guard said two tankers struck mines near Khasab and the Houthis claimed a hit on Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura refinery.

*Why it matters:* The market is trading a geopolitical premium inside a structural surplus, which is why PVM puts the range at \$88–97 rather than a breakout.

### 📊 Money & Policy

**Public debt at 62.2 % of GDP and the highest average interest rate since 2009**

Colombia's EMBI spread rose from 188 to 267 basis points between 7 August and this week, and on 25 November the yield on peso TES maturing in 2036 moved from 12.9 % to 13.25 % while country risk hit its highest level since 7 April.

*Why it matters:* Colombia is again paying more than Mexico or Brazil for dollar debt, and the fiscal rule committee has warned that without adjustment the debt path turns explosive.

<sub>El Tiempo / El País América · 26.11.2026 — [read the source](https://www.eltiempo.com/economia/sectores/riesgo-pais-vuelve-a-maximos-desde-abril-y-a-colombia-le-vuelven-a-cobrar-mas-por-prestarle-3604715)</sub>

**Core PCE at 3.3 % and GDP at 4.3 %: the Fed is priced to hike, not cut**

US core PCE rose 3.3 % year on year in October against 3.2 % expected, headline PCE reached 3.8 %, the highest since 2023, and third-quarter GDP was revised up to 4.3 % from 3.5 %.

*Why it matters:* The rate path has flipped from cuts to hikes in one quarter, and AI equity is where the repricing shows first: the same higher discount rate that hurts long-duration tech also raises the cost of the debt-funded.

<sub>Reuters / CME FedWatch · 26.11.2026 — [read the source](https://www.reuters.com/markets/)</sub>

### 🌍 Geopolitics & Conflict

**Schleswig-Holstein funds the Brunsbüttel hydrogen pipeline with €5.8 million**

Schleswig-Holstein is providing around €5.8 million from the federal infrastructure and climate-neutrality fund for a hydrogen pipeline to Brunsbüttel; the state secretary handed over the grant notice on 26 November.

*Why it matters:* Brunsbüttel is one of the designated import landing points, so this is the inland counterpart to the port side.
