## 📊 The government starts the fiscal adjustment: COP 13.8 trillion of cuts and a COP 634.95 trillion budget by decree

COP 3 trillion of operating costs, COP 4 trillion of debt-service savings from the stronger peso and COP 6.8 trillion of contracts that will not be executed — and formally issued the 2027 budget by decree at COP 634.95 trillion (30 % of GDP): COP 391.4 trillion for operating costs, COP 155.4 trillion for debt service and only COP 88.1 trillion (4.2 % of GDP) for investment.
