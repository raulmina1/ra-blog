## 🤖 Google prices the cheap tier: one model, 1,000 questions, \$0.10 per million input tokens

Google released the Gemini Decisions API built on Gemini Decision 1, which evaluates up to 1,000 questions in a single pass at \$0.10 per million input tokens — roughly one-fifteenth of Gemini 4 Hydrogen's input price. It is a classification/routing model, not a chat model: it returns decisions rather than prose.

**Why it matters.** This is the layer that decides how much of an agent pipeline ever reaches an expensive model — filtering, triage, routing, yes/no gates. For a vault with dozens of cron workers, a \$0.10 classifier is the difference between sending every note to a reasoning model and sending only the 10 % that needs one.

### 🤖 AI & Models

**Alibaba attacks on price: Qwen4-Max at \$4.50 output, 91 % under GPT-6 Astra**

Alibaba launched Qwen4-Max at \$1.50 / \$4.50 per million tokens (cache read \$0.075), 25 % below Qwen3.8-Max and, on list prices read on 16.11, 91 % below GPT-6 Astra (\$10/\$50), ~89 % below Fable 5.2 (\$40 output) and 55 % below GPT-6.1 Sol and Gemini 4 Carbon (\$10 output.

*Why it matters:* The frontier gap is now measured in dollars per task, not index points: Alibaba claims cost per task down 62 % (\$4.43 → \$1.67) on its Work360 set.

### ⚡ Energy & Markets

**First session after the reopen: the surplus argument wins, and Washington adds tankers**

Monday morning: Brent \$95.37 (−\$0.47, −0.49 %), WTI \$88.33 (−\$0.23), extending Friday's settlement of \$95.84 / \$88.56. The driver is the IEA's Q4 call: supply averaging 105 mb/d against 103.3 mb/d of demand (+1.7 mb/d surplus), with October already in surplus by ~2 mb/d and a 52 mb stock build.

*Why it matters:* The surplus is now a forecast with a date and a number, and the diesel tranche is the part that matters for Raúl's refining models: 15 mb of distillate is aimed exactly at the crack that has stayed hot while crude fell.

<sub>Reuters / CNBC / CNA / Economic Times · 16.11.2026 — [read the source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/oil-prices-fall-on-expectations-of-coming-supply-surplus.html)</sub>

**The Länder put the CCfD quota, the grid refinancing and RFNBO rules on the federal government**

The Energieministerkonferenz resolved on 13 November to develop Germany's hydrogen market as the EU's lead market and made six concrete demands: a refinancing mechanism for hydrogen distribution grids plus a fast EnWG amendment implementing the EU gas-market package.

*Why it matters:* This is the first document that names the three things the ramp-up is actually waiting for: a CCfD quota with money behind it, a way to refinance converted distribution grids.

<sub>Energieministerkonferenz, Beschluss Göttingen 13.11.2026 (enmk.de) · 13.11.2026 — [read the source](https://www.enmk.de/documents/enmk_2026_goettingen_beschluss_wasserstoffmarkt.pdf)</sub>

### 📊 Money & Policy

**The AI trade survived its own news: best week since August, and now the print**

Despite Nvidia falling about 13 % on the week and more than 30 % in six weeks (~\$2trn of market value, last around \$160), the major averages had their best week since August and the S&P 500 tech sector rose over 2 %, snapping a five-week slump, as money rotated into Apple, AMD and Broadcom.

*Why it matters:* "The Nvidia story is now idiosyncratic" (Nationwide) is the sentence that matters: the index decoupled from its largest component, which is new behaviour for this cycle.
