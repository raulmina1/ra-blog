## 🤖 Anthropic's own alignment team argues against per-user agents

The team built four simulated settings where people compete for a shared resource (API token budget, clinic calendar, group order, merge queue against a release cutoff) and compared one shared coordinator agent against a team where every user has their own agent messaging the others. The coordinator won all four environments for both Claude models tested, by roughly 10 to 45 percentage points: in the shared-budget case it reached 64 % of the best possible outcome with Opus 5 against 30 % for the per-user team.

**Why it matters.** For a vault full of ~50 single-purpose agents this is a direct design warning: giving every actor its own agent degrades coordination and honesty, not just efficiency. Multi-agent layouts need a shared coordinator and a common state, not a swarm of agents that negotiate by message.

### 🤖 AI & Models

**Gartner puts 2027 AI spending at \$4 trillion, 59 % of it infrastructure**

Gartner forecasts total AI spending of \$4.05trn in 2027 (+47.5 %), of which AI infrastructure \$2.397trn (+53 %, 59 % of the total); AI-optimised servers alone go from \$527.6bn (2026) to \$957.2bn, driven by hyperscalers, neoclouds and "memflation".

*Why it matters:* The ratio is the story: for every dollar of AI spending, roughly 20 cents go to software and models, the rest to concrete, power and chips.

**Anthropic ships self-hosted agents: the credentials never leave the customer's cloud**

Claude Managed Agents entered private beta as a self-hosted edition: the agent harness, sandboxes, the PostgreSQL session store and the workers all run inside the customer's own cloud or on-prem.

*Why it matters:* The compliance objection that killed most European enterprise agent projects ("session data and secrets in a US vendor's cloud") is now technically answerable.

<sub>Anthropic (private beta, via AI Weekly) · 12.11.2026 — [read the source](https://aiweekly.co/ai-news-today)</sub>

**The tooling war moves to "fewer round trips": Code Mode, Nod, /teamwork**

Claude Code v2.1.323 shipped an opt-in Code Mode: instead of invoking its tools one at a time, the model writes JavaScript that calls its own tools, turning ten round trips into one. n8n released Nod, an open-source automation agent (Apache 2.0) that runs on your own machine.

*Why it matters:* The bottleneck being attacked is no longer model quality but loop overhead: fewer calls per task, orchestration on the client side, local execution.

### ⚡ Energy & Markets

**Gascade starts the 115 km "Hyrow" line, and the network will nearly triple before the molecules exist**

Gascade is building Hyrow, a roughly 115 km hydrogen pipeline in Mecklenburg-Vorpommern linking the Rostock area via Wrangelsburg to the north–south axis of the "Flow – making hydrogen happen" programme, with connections for producers and consumers along the route.

*Why it matters:* Germany is building the grid before the supply: BNetzA approved the 9,040 km Kernnetz at €18.9bn for 2032, and the state has promised to carry most of the bill if customers never arrive.

### 📊 Money & Policy

**El Niño could run to May 2027, and the government is already rationing demand**

The government said on 12.11. that El Niño could extend until May 2027, based on the updated NOAA climate outlook, and kept its energy-preparedness measures in place while asking households and industry to reduce water, electricity and gas consumption.

*Why it matters:* Reservoir levels are the input that decides Colombian power prices and, through gas-fired backup, the gas balance Ecopetrol is now trying to fix with imports.

### 🌍 Geopolitics & Conflict

**Gulf states take the Hormuz file to the Security Council: 7,000 missiles, 1,600 ships disrupted**

At a Security Council session requested by Bahrain, the GCC stated that Iran has fired more than 7,000 missiles and drones at Gulf territory since February, naming airports, ports, oil and gas facilities, power stations, desalination plants and residential areas.

*Why it matters:* The war is being formalised into a legal architecture the Gulf states can use (2817 plus a seat at the table), while the physical metric that matters for freight and insurance, transits at −65 %, has not improved at all.
