## ⚡ The government puts its own number on the Hormuz shock: LNG +162 %, diesel +100 %, crude +50 %

In a written answer published with Tuesday's Bundestag papers, the federal government confirmed price increases caused by the situation at the Strait of Hormuz of up to 162 % for LNG, 100 % for diesel and 50 % for crude oil. The document (Drs.

**Why it matters.** Until now the German price debate ran on market quotes (TTF, DAX, pump prices). A government figure of +162 % on LNG is the input that will be used to justify both the reserve levy and any further relief package — which means the fiscal cost of the Hormuz war is about to be booked in Berlin, not only in Brussels.

### 🤖 AI & Models

**Google open-sources TorchTPU, co-developed with Meta — PyTorch now runs natively on TPUs**

At PyTorch Conference 2026 in San Jose, Google released TorchTPU as open source: a native PyTorch backend for TPUs, co-developed with Meta, that removes the JAX/torch_xla detour.

*Why it matters:* This is the strongest software attack on Nvidia's real moat, which is CUDA lock-in rather than silicon.

### ⚡ Energy & Markets

**Brent fails at \$111.99 and settles \$108.79; a 4.93 mb US crude draw meets an SPR at a 44-year low**

Brent for December settled 76 cents or 0.69 % lower at \$108.79 after spiking to \$111.99, a five-month high, on reports of a Qatari-Pakistani mediation proposal; WTI for November expired \$1.87 or 1.8 % lower at \$101.79 after a session high of \$110.48, and the December WTI contract settled at \$97.82.

*Why it matters:* The intraday reversal was diplomacy, the draw was physics.

### 📊 Money & Policy

**Congress passes the 2027 budget at 3:47 a.m. — COP 634.9 trillion, and the JEP loses 23 % of its funding**

The Chamber of Representatives approved the 2027 budget at 3:47 a.m. with 112 votes in favour and one against, adopting the Senate's text (57–2) verbatim, so no conciliation was needed and the government avoided governing by decree.

*Why it matters:* Passing the budget removed the decree risk the market had been pricing, but the arithmetic did not change: debt service is now roughly 46 % of spending and the consolidation depends entirely on a law that has not been.

**Earnings carry the tape: Dow +1.02 %, S&P +0.28 %, Nasdaq flat, and the 10-year slips to 5.12 %**

The Dow rose 529.85 points or 1.02 % to 52,495.88 and the S&P 500 added 0.28 % to 7,712.57, while the Nasdaq slipped 0.02 % to 26,424.82 — a rotation, not a rally.

*Why it matters:* Three inputs are now pointing the same way for the first time in weeks: oil off its high, yields down, hike odds collapsing.

**The IMF and World Bank open a debt-management mission as August activity contracts 0.04 % and gas supply becomes the binding constraint**

An IMF-World Bank technical mission on public-debt management began on Tuesday and runs to 29 October, feeding the 2027–2030 medium-term debt strategy and the 2027 financing plan; gross debt stood at about 60.6 % of GDP in August.

*Why it matters:* Three constraints now bind at once: a fiscal rule suspended, a gas balance that cannot be closed with imports the state cannot buy, and an export sector losing competitiveness to its own strong currency.

### 🌍 Geopolitics & Conflict

**Araghchi: "The Strait of Hormuz is closed" — indirect talks continue through Pakistan and Qatar, and no enriched material leaves Iran**

Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said Iran and the US remain in indirect talks through Pakistan and Qatar, that Tehran presented a seven-day initiative under which the strait would fully reopen on the sixth day.

*Why it matters:* ⏱️ The negotiating structure is now clear and it is sequenced, not priced: Iran wants money and sanctions relief before reopening, Washington wants the waterway first.
