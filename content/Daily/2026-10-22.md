## ⚡ Brent settles at \$108.82, then jumps to \$110.11 on the Saudi escalation

Brent \$108.82 (+0.03 %), near a five-month high, WTI \$97.15 (−0.7 %) — after both benchmarks had gained more than \$2 intraday and then reversed on reports of Iranian-Pakistani mediation. Thursday morning Brent rose \$1.29 (+1.2 %) to \$110.11 and WTI \$0.97 to \$98.12 on the Shaybah strike and the US diplomatic drawdown.
